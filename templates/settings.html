Secondary Fallback

Direct YouTube & YouTube Music audio extraction with candidate scoring & Topic channel prioritization.

YouTube Cookies (cookies.txt)
Checking...

Provide a cookies.txt file so yt-dlp can authenticate with YouTube and bypass sign-in or bot detection restrictions.

Step-by-Step: Exporting YouTube cookies.txt
  1. Install a cookie export extension such as Get cookies.txt LOCALLY (available for Chrome/Firefox/Brave).
  2. Log in to youtube.com in your browser.
  3. Click the extension icon on the YouTube tab.
  4. Click Export or Export As cookies.txt to save the file.
  5. Upload the file above, or place it at /config/cookies.txt in your Docker container mount!

Album Artwork & Cover Art

Control how album covers are saved in directory folders and embedded inside audio files.

Automatically downloads high-resolution artwork into the album directory as cover.jpg or folder.jpg (Navidrome-compatible).
Embeds front cover image bytes into FLAC Picture blocks and MP3/M4A ID3 tags during download.
Song Identification & Audio Matching

Sonarr-style verification parameters to eliminate mismatched, live, or wrong versions of songs.

Compare downloaded audio duration against the official release and reject candidates that differ. Disable this to accept any audio file that downloads (e.g. for remixes, live versions, or when official track durations are unavailable).
If downloaded audio fails strict duration and tag verification, discard it immediately rather than keeping the incorrect song.
Monitors the /music storage directory for deleted or relocated audio files in the background and keeps the database in sync.
